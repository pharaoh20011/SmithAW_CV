About Us

Your Neighborhood Landscaping Partner

Tell-A-Friend Landscaping is a locally owned, African American-owned small business taking pride in delivering quality workmanship, dependable schedules, and neighborly service to the Kansas City metro area.

Founded on the values of hard work and community pride, we believe a beautiful lawn brings neighbors together. That's why we focus on establishing long-term friendships with our clients by consistently showing up on time, keeping pricing competitive, and maintaining an meticulous eye for detail. Whether you have a small residential lawn in Raytown or a large corporate property in Overland Park, we treat your grounds like they're our own.
