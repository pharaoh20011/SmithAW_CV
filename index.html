About Me

I am a modern UI/UX and Front-End Developer & tech specialist with a professional background spanning several Fortune 500 companies. My career bridges the gap between enterprise IT customer support, modern user interface development, and advanced prompt engineering. Backed by a strong foundation as a Sr. SAS Help Desk Representative and team lead, I started my journey with an A+ Certification and hands-on IT support training in Washington, DC, before earning a Graphic Design certificate from Miami Dade College. I have since upskilled with modern certifications in Meta Front-End Development, WordPress, and AWS Data Analytics, while integrating advanced prompt engineering techniques into my technical toolkit. I am now seeking a collaborative, remote Jr. Prompt Engineer or Jr. Front-End Developer position or Internship, where I can continue to build intuitive user interfaces and leverage my advanced troubleshooting skills to drive user satisfaction as part of a team.

Outside of my professional life, I am a devoted father who is incredibly proud of raising a kind, strong, and independent daughter. I am also an avid traveler and explorer, having lived across several American cities, Europe, and South America. When I am not coding or reading, you can find me riding my motorcycle, hiking, camping, grilling, or listening to classical, jazz, blues, and R&B music.
