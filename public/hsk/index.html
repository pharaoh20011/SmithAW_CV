About the Chef

Chef Marcus Johnson brings heartfelt Southern hospitality, refined technique, and bold flavor to every table. His menus are inspired by family traditions, fresh ingredients, and a passion for gathering people together through food.

As an African American chef, he proudly honors the rich culinary legacy that shaped his journey while presenting soul food with modern elegance for today’s celebrations.
