Cast of Characters

Greg Garrelson

Founding tri-partner/COO and top investment manager at Pimico (Professional Investment Management Innovation Corporation).

Carry Mitchelson

Greg’s new personal assistant, described as a stunning blonde bombshell with captivating green eyes, well-built, long wavy blonde hair, strong back, nice-shaped thighs, well-rounded butt, and smooth legs.

Veronica LaFayette

Successful singer-songwriter with a mesmerizing voice and striking dark hair, blue eyes, and a 1920s bob haircut. Highly intelligent and deeply passionate.

David Michaels

Greg’s best friend, CEO, and founding tri-partner at Pimico.

Tessa Garrelson

Greg’s younger sister, close in age, described as important to Greg and David’s past.

Erica Stamson

Greg’s ex-girlfriend, described as a longtime consort who leaves Greg, triggering his downward spiral.

Janet Thompson

Greg’s longtime personal assistant, described as highly competent and reliable.

Molly Kierson

David’s personal assistant, described as a beautiful, medium dark-skinned woman in her early 30s, long dark hair, dark brown eyes, petite frame.

Dr. Darren Michaels

David’s twin brother, a doctor at the clinic, tall with dark hair, light green eyes, stout build, short full face beard.

Hello It's George

Born and raised in Southwest Kansas. A graduate of Protection High School in 1986, serving in the U.S. Navy from 86-95, traveling the world and experiencing the vibrancy and differences of many cultures around the globe as well as seeing many historical sites. He moved to Kansas City in 99, marrying his wife Carmen in August 2001.

The Reform Party of Kansas nominated him for United States Senate – Kansas in both 2002 and 2004. Following those campaigns for public office, George and his wife Carmen focused on family, and raising their 4 children.

This led to the writing of the first short story - I had a bad dream on a dark, stormy Night. As the children expressed their ideas for what they wanted to become, more short stories were written. Finally, there were enough short stories to combine into a small book - Adventures in Dreamland - published in May 2022.

After publishing his first book, he began to work on other story ideas. What started as a love triangle concept in 1988, morphed into a trilogy of novels. Thirty-One months of writing labor, even after having a stroke, finally brought forth book one of the trilogy - A Chance Encounter - published in November 2025. Currently, he is working on book two of the trilogy - A Random Encounter.

George H Cook Book Author & Publisher
